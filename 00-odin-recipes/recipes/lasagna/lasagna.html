Description

The image shows a golden, bubbling lasagna fresh from the oven, with several visible layers of tender pasta, rich tomato-and-beef sauce, creamy cheese, and melted mozzarella. The top is lightly browned and crisp around the edges, while the center looks soft, cheesy, and delicious.

Ingredients

  • 12 lasagna sheets
  • 500 g ground beef
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 700 g tomato passata or crushed tomatoes
  • 2 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1 teaspoon dried basil
  • 500 g ricotta cheese
  • 1 egg
  • 100 g grated Parmesan cheese
  • 250 g shredded mozzarella
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 190°C (375°F).
  2. Heat the olive oil in a large pan. Add the onion and cook for 3–4 minutes until softened.
  3. Add the garlic and ground beef. Cook until the beef is browned, breaking it apart as it cooks.
  4. Stir in the tomato paste, crushed tomatoes, oregano, basil, salt, and pepper. Simmer for about 15–20 minutes.
  5. In a bowl, mix the ricotta, egg, half of the Parmesan, and a pinch of salt and pepper.
  6. Spread a thin layer of meat sauce over the bottom of a baking dish.
  7. Add a layer of lasagna sheets, followed by some ricotta mixture, meat sauce, and mozzarella.
  8. Repeat the layers until all the ingredients are used, finishing with meat sauce, mozzarella, and the remaining Parmesan.
  9. Cover the dish with foil and bake for 25 minutes.
  10. Remove the foil and bake for another 15–20 minutes, until the cheese is bubbling and golden brown.
  11. Let the lasagna rest for 10–15 minutes before slicing and serving.
