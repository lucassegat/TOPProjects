Homemade Bread

Description

The image shows a freshly baked loaf of bread with a beautifully golden-brown crust and a soft, fluffy interior. The loaf has a few shallow cuts across the top, giving it a rustic homemade appearance. When sliced, the bread has an airy texture and is perfect for sandwiches, toast, or serving with butter.

Ingredients

  • 500 g all-purpose or bread flour
  • 7 g active dry yeast
  • 1½ teaspoons salt
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • 300 ml warm water
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil or melted butter

Steps

  1. In a large bowl, combine the warm water, sugar, and yeast. Let it sit for 5–10 minutes until foamy.
  2. Add the flour, salt, and olive oil to the yeast mixture.
  3. Mix until a rough dough forms.
  4. Transfer the dough to a lightly floured surface and knead for 8–10 minutes, until smooth and elastic.
  5. Place the dough in a lightly oiled bowl, cover it with a clean towel, and let it rise for about 1–1½ hours, or until doubled in size.
  6. Punch down the dough gently and shape it into a loaf.
  7. Place the loaf on a baking sheet or in a greased loaf pan. Cover and let it rise again for 30–45 minutes.
  8. Preheat the oven to 220°C (425°F).
  9. Make a few shallow cuts across the top of the loaf with a sharp knife.
  10. Bake for 25–30 minutes, until the bread is golden brown and sounds hollow when tapped.
  11. Let the bread cool on a wire rack before slicing and serving.
